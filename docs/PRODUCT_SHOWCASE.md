# 🧬 Nirvana Smart-Chain — Product Visual Showcase & Documentation

## 📸 Product Interface Gallery

### 1. Web3 Gateway & Landing Interface (`Nirvana Health Chain`)
The portal entry point welcoming manufacturers, distributors, retail dispensaries, and regulators into the decentralized supply chain.

![Nirvana Health Chain Landing](images/01-nirvana-health-chain-hero.png)

- **Purpose**: Decentralized identity authentication and role-based portal routing.
- **Key Elements**: Cyber-biomedical design system, single-click entry, and secure stakeholder credential verification.

---

### 2. Retail Pharmacy Operations & Analytics Dashboard
Central command center for retail pharmacists and dispensary managers.

![Pharmacy Dashboard](images/02-pharmacy-dashboard.png)

- **Telemetry & Insights**:
  - **Stock Inventory**: Real-time SKU counters, available units, and bay shelf organization.
  - **Valuation & Purchases**: Dynamic total stock valuation (e.g. ₹6,10,992.10) and restocking expense tracking.
  - **Order Pipeline**: Breakdown of paid vs. pending stock orders.
  - **Analytical Visualizations**: Interactive monthly trends and categorical sales distributions.

---

### 3. Dispensary Storage & Warehouse Shelf Kiosk
Interactive visual representation of physical medicine shelves, bays, and batches.

![Dispensary Warehouse Kiosk](images/03-dispensary-warehouse-kiosk.png)

- **Spatial Organization**: Direct mapping between digital inventory and physical dispensary shelf bays (Bay 1 through Bay 5).
- **Interactive Catalog**: Search by active formulations, chemical ingredients, or specific storage racks.
- **3D Visualizations**: Modern 3D rendering for rapid medicine identification and package verification.

---

### 4. Smart Settlement & Dual-Camera QR Verification Protocol
Zero-trust shipment handover protocol guaranteeing genuine medicine delivery and cryptographic payment execution.

![Blockchain Shipment Settlement](images/04-blockchain-shipment-settlement.png)

- **Nirvana Smart Coin (NSC)**: Live balance monitoring, whitelist verification, and allowance authorization.
- **Dual-Stream QR Scanner**: Instant hardware switching between live **Browser Webcam** and **Network IP Webcam** feeds.
- **Cart Verification**: Auto-populates encrypted Cart ID, Wholesaler address, Dispatch date, and Manufacturer identity upon scanning.
- **Settlement Execution**: One-click settlement triggering both blockchain verification and automated gateway payment.
